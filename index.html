2 Sound

·

What the game sends voice with. Steam voice is today's; the rest of the first group are its older codecs.

vaudio_miles is not a TF2 codec. The Source engines of 2006 and 2007 could run it; current TF2 ships its files and refuses the name. It has not been checked against any recording of a running engine. Everything after the receiver's gain is TF2's of today.

A state of the player, with no variable: the whole sound passes the game's water preset (14).

Every stage of the voice path, in signal order; the app's own extras last.
A Sender

Which channel the game's microphone gets. Left is what one stereo cable gave it; a mono file is not affected.

How long the sender's Steam had run: its 0.4 s hold after speech is timed on a clock that coarsens with it.

B Network

The share of voice packets that never arrive. Not the game's own number: its net_fakeloss 5 loses about 22% of them.

Late packets. Fitted at 50 ms, where 3.2% of frames were late; other values are scaled from that.

C Receiver

Where the auto-gain aims each block, from its mean (0) to its peak (1). The game's default is 0.5.

The most the receiver's auto-gain may amplify. The game's default is 10.

The game's voice_scale. Not a plain volume: below 1 it lowers the voice by more than its number.

D Listener room & output

The game's volume setting; it sets the loudest the file can get. 1.0 is the game's default. The recordings behind the model were made at 0.15.

The rate of the listener's sound device; the PC converts the game's 44.1 kHz sound to it. 48000 Hz is usual.

The automatic room's measures (dsp_room 1)

What the map would measure around the listener, in units (inches); open sides count only under the sky.

+ Extras: not in the game

Loudness into the game. Above 1 the sender clips, as an overdriven microphone does.

Not in the game: a filter before the codec. 0 is off.

Not in the game: a filter before the codec. 20000 is off.

Whether the sender sends only while you speak. Auto is the codec's own: on for Opus, off for the vaudio_ ones.

The level that opens the voice gate, in dBFS. Steam's own is -40; blank is the codec's own.

For comparison only: the sound goes to the receiver without being coded.

16 is the codec's own bitrate; 8 is half of it. vaudio_speex and vaudio_miles each have one bitrate and ignore this.

How much voice one network packet carries. A lost packet loses all of it. Steam voice sends each of its messages as one packet and ignores this.

The receiver's auto-gain as the game runs it. Off is for comparison only: the voice at unity gain.

The game plays some phrases early after a short silence. Which ones is random.

Plays the live monitor sooner by cutting part of each phrase's start. Not how TF2 sounds.

The custom room (dsp_room 99)

The app's own room, not the game's. At a high mix its 32-bit sums can wrap, as some of the game's rooms do fully wet.
