# W3-18: Speex 1.0.2 with the arithmetic of the game's module, equal to the game's recordings

> **Status 2026-11-12: built and merged** at `e4c3d73` (cache v29, pull request #23). Report: `docs/reports/W3-18-speex-exact.md`.

**Depends on:** W3-12 (built), W3-17c merged (it changes `tests/render-stream.verify.mjs` and `sw.js`). **Kind:**
code. `tf-builder` (effort high), then `tf-reviewer`. **Branch:** `claude/w3-18-speex-exact`, from `main`.
**Worktree:** given in the brief. **Time budget:** 5 hours. One part.

## Why

The owner (2026-11-11): the other three codecs equal the game to the sample, so Speex should too.

A recording of the game's Speex exists since Set M (`tests/REFERENCE_2026.md`): HL2DM's 32-bit game, file input,
912 frames at `voice_maxgain 1` and 913 at `voice_maxgain 10`. The app's build of Speex 1.0.2 (the library's
sources unchanged, compiled strictly) gives that recording in 92,967 of 145,920 samples: the same sound, coded
differently from one frame on.

An investigation (2026-11-11; private, summarised here) found why and how to close it:

- The module's codec arithmetic is SSE2 in single precision, not x87. It was compiled with the compiler's fast
  floating-point model, so **25 places in 7 files of libspeex 1.0.2 do not compute what strict C computes**: sums
  added eight at a time, three double expressions computed in float, two divisions turned into multiplications by
  a reciprocal, a few re-ordered additions, and four calls that are the float variants of the C library's
  functions (`cosf`, `acosf`, `expf`, `logf`).
- A copy of libspeex 1.0.2 with those 25 places rewritten in plain C, compiled strictly, gives the game's gain-1
  recording in **145,920 of 145,920** samples and, after the app's gain law, the gain-10 recording in **146,080 of
  146,080**; natively and as WebAssembly with this repository's recipe.
- The module was then run in a CPU emulator (it equals the game's recording there, 145,920 of 145,920, and executes
  no x87 instruction), and used as the reference on synthetic signals: 255,746 frames. The patched build equals it
  in every packet and sample **provided the float cosine and arc cosine are the C runtime's own**, which are not
  correctly rounded (one unit off on 34 of 1,200,002 and 1,928 of 1,300,003 random arguments). With a correctly
  rounded stand-in the build still equals both recordings but parts from the module in about one stretch per
  250,000 frames (an hour of sound).
- No dependence on the listener's processor was found.

Why the double functions are enough for five of the seven math calls (measured in the investigation): `expf` only
ever gets the 10 entries of the two gain tables, and on all 10 the module's result is the nearest float; `logf`
only picks the nearest entry of a gain table, and on every float within 30,000 floats of each of the 8 decision
points the two pick the same entry; the double `exp` and `cos` values the codec uses (538 fixed ones) lie at least
383,104 double units from a float rounding boundary; `floor(3.5*log(x))` has a margin of 2.26e-9 against a last
place of 3.6e-15.

## Other facts from the investigation, for the README and the tests where cheap

- The module holds samples until 160 are there; a final call pads with zeros; packets do not depend on how calls
  are split (already the framer's behaviour; keep the tests).
- Reset: the encoder equals a new codec from there; the decoder's frames equal a new decoder's in 136 of 138 on
  the speech signal (part of its state survives), which the speechReset hash above pins.
- A zero frame on a new codec gives the packet `1e9d66000039ce70001ce738000e739c000739ce` and silence.
- Decompress: fewer than 20 bytes give nothing; 21 to 39 give one frame; a 20-byte packet of `0xff` gives 160
  samples without touching the decoder's state. A sub-mode 0 frame and a lost frame draw noise from the C
  runtime's `rand()`, which belongs to the game's thread and cannot be reproduced from packets: out of scope (the
  app plays a lost frame as zeros).
- Engine quality to bytes per frame: 0 and 1: 6; 2: 15; 3: 20; 4: 28; 5: 38. Only quality 3 is the game's and
  only that setting is claimed exact.
