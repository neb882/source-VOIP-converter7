[the order](../work-orders/AC-02-suite-over-seeds.md) asks. Tests and documents only: no file under `engine/` or

## What changed, file by file

| File | Change |
| --- | --- |
| `tests/accuracy.mjs` | A `--seeds <n>` option. With no option or `--seeds 1` the code path is the one on `main`, untouched. With `n` above 1, `measureOverSeeds` measures each row at the row's own seed (`settings.seed`, taken as the engine takes it) or the default, `0xC0FFEE` (read, `engine/render.js`), then at seeds 1 to `n − 1`, skipping any that equals the first. Each measurement is one whole `measureInPage` call. The first measurement passes the row's settings as they stand, so it is the one the suite makes without the option; the others add `seed`. The printed row: the median over the seeds, then `[least…most]`, for the clock, the alignment r, the level rms and worst, the level r and the offset; the median only for the phrases (Spurts), the pieces (Segs), the delay's least and greatest, the two step counts and the clipped shares; the worst band as the band of the median seed's measurement with the median of its value. Two lines above the header say so. The JSON row holds `seeds: [{ seed, timing, summary, ...result }]` and a `summary` with `{ median, least, most }` for each figure (the worst band adds its `hz` and the `seed` it came from); the top level gains `seeds: n`. A seed that fails is kept in `seeds` with its `error`, the row is summed over the others and says which seed failed, and the run exits 1. `--write-predictions`, `--score-predictions` and `--simulate-takes` refuse `--seeds` with a message and exit 1, before anything is rendered. `--seeds` that is not a whole number of 1 or more is refused the same way. The header comment and the usage line say all this. |
| `tests/README.md` | The accuracy row gains one sentence on `--seeds <n>`. |
| `docs/ACCURACY.md` | "How to read each column" gains one item: when the table comes from a run with `--seeds`, each figure is the median over the seeds, with its range after it for the clock, the alignment r, the level and the offset. The table and everything below it are not touched. |
| `docs/reports/AC-02-suite-over-seeds.md` | This report. |

## Choices the order left open

- **A row whose own seed is one of 1 to `n − 1`.** The order says to measure such a row at its own seed, then at
  seeds 1 to `n − 1`, skipping the one equal to the first. Read as written, that row gets `n − 1` measurements, not
  `n`; the builder kept the rule as written (chosen). No row of `tests/accuracy.takes.json` gives a seed, so every
  row there gets `n`. *Changed in the fix round: such a row gets `n` different seeds.*
- **The median of an even count** is the mean of the two middle values; the median seed of the worst band is the
  lower of the two middle ones by its value (chosen). The orchestrator's run, at 9 seeds, has an odd count.
  *Changed in the fix round: the median seed is chosen by the band's size, and its own value is printed.*
- **The delay and the clipped shares**, which the order does not name, print their medians only (chosen); the JSON's
  `summary` has their ranges.
